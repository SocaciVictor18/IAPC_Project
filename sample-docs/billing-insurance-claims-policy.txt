BILLING AND INSURANCE CLAIMS POLICY
Owner: Billing & Insurance department - Confidential

1. Approval of claims
Insurance claims up to 2,000 EUR are approved by the billing clerk.
Claims between 2,000 EUR and 10,000 EUR require approval by the Billing Manager.
Claims above 10,000 EUR require written approval by the Head of Billing and the Chief Financial Officer.

2. Patient invoices
Invoices may only contain the minimum medical information needed by the insurer (diagnosis code, procedure code, date).

3. Segregation of duties
The person who creates a claim may never approve the same claim.
