MEDICAL DEVICE SECURITY PROCEDURE
Owner: Biomedical Engineering and IT Security - Version 1.2

1. Inventory
All connected medical devices (infusion pumps, monitors, imaging systems) are registered in the medical device inventory
with their location, owner, software version and network address.

2. Network
Medical devices are placed in a separate network segment and cannot reach the internet directly.

3. Patching
Security patches approved by the manufacturer are installed once a year during planned maintenance.
Devices that can no longer be patched are isolated or replaced.

4. Incidents
Any malfunction that may be caused by a cyber attack is reported immediately to IT Security and to the device manufacturer.
