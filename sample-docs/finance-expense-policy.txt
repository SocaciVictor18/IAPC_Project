FINANCE OPERATIONS - EXPENSE APPROVAL POLICY
Owner: Finance Operations (FIN) - Confidential

1. Approval thresholds
Expenses up to 500 EUR are approved by the team lead.
Expenses between 500 EUR and 5,000 EUR require approval by the Finance Operations manager.
Expenses above 5,000 EUR require written approval by the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

2. Corporate cards
Corporate cards may only be used for business expenses. Receipts must be uploaded within 10 working days.
Lost corporate cards must be blocked through the bank portal immediately and reported to Finance Operations.

3. Segregation of duties
The person who requests a payment may never be the person who approves it.
Vendor bank account changes must be verified by a call-back to a known phone number before payment.

4. Audit
Finance Operations reviews a random sample of 5% of expense reports every month.
