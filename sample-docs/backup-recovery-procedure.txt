BACKUP AND RECOVERY PROCEDURE
Document owner: IT/DevOps - Version 1.3

1. Scope
This procedure applies to all production databases, file servers and SaaS exports that contain customer or employee data.

2. Backup schedule
Incremental backups are taken every night at 02:00 UTC. Full backups are taken every Sunday.
Backups are encrypted with AES-256 before leaving the production environment and are stored in a separate cloud region.
Backup retention is 35 days for daily backups and 12 months for monthly backups.

3. Restore testing
A restore test of one critical system is performed once per year and the result is recorded in the change log.
Restore tests must verify data integrity and measure the recovery time against the target RTO of 4 hours.

4. Responsibilities
IT/DevOps runs the backups and the restore tests. The Security Team reviews the backup logs monthly.

5. Logging
Backup job logs are kept for 90 days in the central logging platform.
